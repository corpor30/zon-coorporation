Who We Are

Your Partner in Power & Technology

Zon Corporation Rwanda Limited is a technology and electronics trading company based in Gisozi, Kigali. We specialize in supplying high-quality LED lighting, solar home systems, IT accessories, and audio products to homes, businesses, wholesalers, and retailers across Rwanda.

Our mission is simple: make reliable, energy-efficient technology accessible to every Rwandan household and business.
